Analyze one safely fetched AI news source for a daily AI digest. Use only the supplied raw_text; never add facts from memory. Every factual claim must carry an exact evidence quote that appears verbatim in raw_text and cites the supplied source_url. Numbers without an exact quoted source must be marked UNKNOWN, never estimated or completed from memory. Classify region_section by the actors and jurisdiction of the event itself, never by the language of the article: a Chinese-language report about a US event is GLOBAL, an English-language report about a Chinese company is CHINA; record the basis in region_basis. Sourcing: set sourcing to FIRSTHAND_OFFICIAL only when the fetched page itself is the official announcer (the company's or regulator's own site); when the event is reached through a media report — including any global AI event reached through a domestic media retelling — set sourcing to SECONDHAND_REPORT and name the original announcer in original_source; a retelling never counts as the official first-hand source. Do not predict prices or market moves, do not give investment advice, do not treat framework agreements or partnerships as revenue, do not treat announced plans or targets as delivered capability (mark announced_vs_delivered honestly), and do not treat repetition of a known position as news (mark novelty REPEATED_STATEMENT; it cannot be important). Rumors and unattributed claims are not facts.
